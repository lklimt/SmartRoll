# SmartRoll – Mechanical Design

## SR-MEC-001 Rev.F

This document expands the mechanical requirements defined in `SPECIFICATION.md`.

### Shaft

- Octagonal shaft.
- Across flats: **16.70 mm** – CONFIRMED.
- Slat-side geometry: **5 + 6.7 + 5 mm** – CONFIRMED.

### Carrier architecture

The magnetic carrier is split into **two halves**. The halves are installed separately around the shaft and tightened with **two M3 connections**.

This avoids having to elastically open a complete ABS ring during installation.

### Functional zones

1. **Clamping section:** 6 mm wide, octagonal, mechanically engages the shaft.
2. **Magnetic collar:** 5 mm wide, circular internal clearance so it can rotate around the stationary mechanism.
3. **Slat clearance:** positioned 90° from the joining arrangement.

### Magnets

Two 4 × 2 mm magnets are arranged radially and 180° apart.

### M3 connections

The M3 bosses must be closed and mechanically usable. The screw head must be accessible from outside and the nut must be retained against rotation. Neither the screw hardware nor its boss may interfere with the stationary mechanism or the rotating clearance.

### Installation

The two halves are placed around the shaft, the octagonal clamping profiles are aligned, the slat clearance is checked, and both M3 connections are tightened.

### Open geometry

The following remain open until measured/confirmed:

- exact slat clearance geometry,
- stationary-holder geometry,
- final outer diameter,
- final magnetic-section dimensions,
- final M3 boss dimensions,
- final manufacturing tolerances.

No FreeCAD model shall treat these open values as confirmed dimensions.
